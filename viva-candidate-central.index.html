What is an EA, really?

Not "admin support." At Viva, an Executive Assistant is a strategic partner to founders and C-suite leaders at fast-growing US and Canadian startups.

You're the person who makes sure nothing important slips — the one keeping their time, focus, and follow-through intact while they build.

Your path to an offer

A real sequence — five stages, in order. Tap each to expand.

1 Submit your application 10–15 min ▸
A short form with your resume, in English. Be honest — it helps everyone move faster.
Before you apply: skim Viva's mission and the EA job description so you can speak to fit right away.
2 Screening assessment 15–20 min ▸
A short video or voice note on your communication style, plus a 1-minute typing test.
Use a computer, keep a steady pace, and be concise — this is your first impression.
3 Group interview 30 min ▸
A group session with the People Team, focused on communication and genuine enthusiasm.
Bring a question or two — curiosity about the role reads as much as your answers do.
4 Skills assessment 2 hrs · 24-hr window ▸
Built to surface a driver mindset, critical thinking, and intuition — how you make judgment calls.
Find a quiet space. Precision and proactive problem-solving matter more than speed here.
5 Final interview 30 min ▸
Roleplay scenarios that ask you to think like the executive you'd be supporting.
Offer more than one solution per scenario, and stay calm — composure is part of the evaluation.
05

Fast answers

How many executives will I support?+
Up to three, from a single company — most EAs support one to two.
Who do I report to as an EA?+
During training, an onboarding buddy supports and guides you. Once you're matched with an executive, a Customer Success Manager becomes your leader — focused specifically on your growth and success.
Is there real growth potential?+
Yes — Viva aims for accelerated promotion, with the Executive Team flagging candidates directly. A typical path looks like:
Executive Assistant→ Senior EA (yr 1–2)→ Chief of Staff I (+2 yrs)→ Chief of Staff II (+2 yrs)
Can I work part-time alongside another job or school?+
No — this is a full-time, exclusive commitment.
What are the actual work hours?+
40–45 hrs/week, mostly 8am–5pm local time, adjustable by up to 2 hours for your executive's needs.
What tech and workspace do I need?+
Internet25 Mbps+ down, 5 Mbps+ up
ComputerWindows 10+ or macOS 10.14+, 8GB RAM+, 1280×720 screen
CameraBuilt-in or USB webcam, 720p HD
AudioSpeakers + mic, built-in, USB, or wireless
WorkspaceQuiet, professional, distraction-free with noise suppression
Do you only hire women?+
No. The mission centers women's empowerment, but the team welcomes qualified candidates of all genders — men already thrive here too.
Will I get a work laptop?+
A company MacBook arrives after your ~8-week probation. You'll need your own computer until then.
